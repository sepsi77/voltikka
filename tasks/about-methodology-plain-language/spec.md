# Plain-language annual estimate methodology

## Goal
Review /tietoa, especially #menetelma, against the implemented pricing rules. Explain in everyday Finnish why increasingly complex electricity contracts cannot always have precise like-for-like annual prices, and which assumptions make comparison possible.

## Scope
Copy, focused rendering tests, and the nearby methodology documentation. Preserve layout, public anchors, action strip, identity/funding/contact facts, dynamic values, and existing uncommitted pricing changes. No pricing-engine changes, production actions, commit, or deployment.

## Acceptance
A visible explanation before the technical detail states that annual figures are comparison estimates, not bill promises. Known prices, estimated future rates, consumption timing, Spot/reset fallbacks, Hybrid effects, short terms, packages, VAT and savings scope are explained without jargon or false guarantees. Examples are clearly illustrative. Focused tests, asset build, and diff checks pass.

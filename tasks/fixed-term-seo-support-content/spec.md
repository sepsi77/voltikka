# Fixed-term SEO support content

Add useful, visible supporting content to the general fixed-term contract listing and the 6-, 12-, and 24-month listing pages. Keep contract discovery as the primary page task. Place the content after the contract results and before the existing related-links section. Keep content specific to each duration and avoid unsupported or stale market claims.

The support section must not be only a long text article. Lead with current, precomputed market data and explain the decision it supports. Compare fully fixed 6-, 12-, and 24-month offered-energy-price medians on one common date and basis. Each duration needs at least 10 contracts and complete p20, median, and p80 values. Translate each difference from the selected duration into euros at the visitor's consumption, before monthly fees. Show the current listing by pricing mechanism and state whether the lowest result is fully fixed, consumption-effect, or market-priced. Show the matching 30-day trend and forecast with a careful euro-equivalent explanation. Keep the range figure as secondary context. Remove repeated list totals and lowest-price facts that the cards already show. Keep labels honest about monthly fees, comparison periods, hybrid base-only estimates, and forecast limits.

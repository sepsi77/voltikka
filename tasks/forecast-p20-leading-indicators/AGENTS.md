## Result

Pooled p20 rolling futures beat their own unchanged/history/intercept controls in both horizons and regimes, but the primary incremental futures improvement over history is smaller than median/p80. P20 gains are not consistent across every term and independent per-term control. B p20-change helps primary pooled median errors, mainly six months; it fails to repeat in per-term fits, older observed fits or the 14-day checks. All pooled primary B models call UP, matching class balance rather than adding direction skill. Spread has an encouraging older 14-day result but not a stable cross-regime gain. See `report.md` for all prespecified comparisons and unfavorable cases.

This period was already inspected. P20 is the 20th percentile, not a cheapest-20% mean or fixed company cohort. Export revisions and offer composition are uncontrolled.

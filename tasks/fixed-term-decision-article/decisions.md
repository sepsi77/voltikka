# Decisions

- The page is an aggregate market guide. It must not embed `ContractTypeComparison` or compare individual offers.
- The primary current evidence compares the open-ended segment's published current fixed rate with fully fixed 6, 12, and 24 month market distributions on one common eligible date. Each row uses p20, median, and p80. The open-ended rate can change later under seller terms. The historical chart stays on 6/12/24 only because older open-ended observed classification can include products that canonical classification now puts in market-reset segments.
- A separate same-date 5,000 kWh annual-cost comparison between open-ended fixed-price and 12-month fully fixed products gives the price of certainty including monthly fees. It uses only the active annual method and the request's expected pricing basis or an explicitly valid mixed aggregate. The open-ended total is a 12-month estimate because the seller can change its price under the terms.
- Historical trends use small multiples or an equivalent clear presentation. Do not overlap three shaded distribution bands in one chart.
- Links to `/sahkosopimus/maaraaikainen` and exact-duration listing pages replace the individual-contract tool.
- Contract duration and pricing method are separate. Market data labelled fully fixed must use the existing fixed-term statistics segments, which exclude Spot, market-reset, and consumption-effect products under current canonical classification.
- Forecasts use `FixedContractPriceForecast::eligibleForPublicDisplay()` and the configured model version/current pricing basis.
- Forecast direction is evidence, not a promise. Confidence, date, horizon, and sample size stay visible.
- Forecast accuracy is not added in this task. Add it only after enough matured forecasts have been evaluated.
- The visual direction inherits Voltikka's calm editorial data style. Most content stays flat on white with slate rules. Coral marks actions and limited attention only.
- `ContractMarketInsightService::fixedTermArticle()` is the one cached article payload. It combines the article-specific four-segment current comparison, the 5,000 kWh annual price-of-certainty result, bounded weekly history, and the latest complete eligible 30-day forecast quantiles. It reads only the two aggregate tables.
- Historical daily evidence is selected before weekly aggregation. In canonical mode, canonical evidence wins when both bases exist on one date. Invalid canonical evidence creates a gap instead of falling back to an observed row on that date. Feature-off mode reads observed evidence only, so shadow canonical rows do not leak into the public page.
- The material rewrite was reviewed on 31.8.2026. The page and Article schema use the fixed `2026-08-31` editorial review date. The latest stored market or forecast date is shown separately and does not change `dateModified`.
- The data-driven HTML title and Article headline omit a year. This prevents a stale literal year in future years.
- The history uses three server-rendered SVG small multiples with one shared scale. Each chart has a native disclosure table with every weekly p20, median, p80, and contract-count point.
- The focused test observes the article payload's database reads before it requests the full page. This isolates the aggregate service guarantee from unrelated shared-layout queries.

Final verification must be repeated after the approved open-ended and annual-cost evidence is added.
- The article current unit comparison is a separate canonical-only payload. It requires open-ended and fixed 6/12/24 rows on one eligible date and does not change the three-duration `fixedTermComparison()` payload used by SEO listings. Feature-off mode fails closed because observed `open_ended` history cannot prove a fully fixed product set.
- The 5,000 kWh price-of-certainty payload is also canonical-only. It uses the active annual method and permits `mixed_evidence` only on the current expected-basis unit endpoint under existing statistics-page semantics. It does not require cross-segment compatibility keys: different estimator methods are inherent to open-ended and fixed-term annual totals, and compatibility keys guard time-series continuity rather than a same-date comparison. Article cache schema v4 invalidates the earlier data-dump payload.

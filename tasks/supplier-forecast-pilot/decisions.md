# Decisions

- This is an offline snapshot-index diagnostic, not a formal retail-premium model or application model replay. No unknown VAT premium is filled.
- Producer review: `ContractPriceStatisticsService::calculateForDate` admits Household/Both/null targets. `cleanValues` admits energy prices from 0.005 through 50.0 c/kWh inclusive. Add a finite-value guard. The segment classifier excludes canonical reset and consumption-effect categories before structural duration classification. Require both stored exact fixed-term segment and FixedPrice enum; exclude Time/Season even when their producer-weighted rates are present.
- Canonical General snapshots hold household VAT-normalized offered unit rates. Historical observed General snapshots use relational General rates. Their scale and population continuity is an explicit assumption, not historical VAT proof. Audience filtering is trusted from the producer; target group is not exported and cannot be re-audited per historical row.
- FixedTermHedgeCostService selects latest FI Base trade date strictly before each date, next full calendar month delivery, month then quarter then year fallback per month, calendar-day weights, settlement / 10 * 1.255. Use exactly this reference rule.
- Full dateful tables are not original as-of query archives. This is date-safe reconstruction with hindsight availability of stored evidence, not proof of deployable contemporaneous performance.
- Supplier medians retain duplicate and campaign/product variant mix. Contract IDs are not lineages. Daily medians and their changes are not independent retail repricing periods.
- Fees are excluded. References are not actual supplier procurement costs. The index difference is not a wholesale physical margin or profit.
- All coefficients and the evaluation interval were specified before this calculation. Do not choose winners or tune parameters after holdout results.
- Completed run: 11,608 eligible snapshots, 891 common evaluation pairs, 51 supplier-term cells, 23 suppliers. Supplier MAE is worse than unchanged under both weighting rules. The pooled-baseline difference is less than 0.0004 c/kWh and changes sign with weighting. Do not adopt the supplier model from this evidence.
- `analysis.py` writes nine audit/result CSVs and `summary.json`. `--check` independently re-reads raw evidence and artifacts, reconstructs medians and calendar-day strips, checks all candidate eligibility decisions and EWMA weights, and recomputes weighted aggregate errors. Both modes passed. Source files remain unchanged.
- `history_median_changes` counts changes between successive accepted daily medians, including across gaps and the method boundary. `median_changes` describes the complete available series through export end, not a training-only count. Neither is a count of genuine independent repricing periods. Per-pair history fields are strictly prior to issue.
- No runtime confidence label, forecast setting, public feature, production operation, or formal premium row was changed. Application changes elsewhere in the working tree belong to other tasks and were not edited here.

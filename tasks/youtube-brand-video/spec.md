# YouTube brand video (10–15 s)

## Goal

Make a 10–15 s video ad for Voltikka with code (HTML frames rendered by a headless browser, encoded with ffmpeg). No Remotion. Code and renders live in `videos/`.

## Brief (user, 2026-10-30)

- **Channel:** YouTube first (in-stream). Primary format 16:9.
- **Audience:** set by Google Ads custom segments from search keywords: people who actively shop for and compare electricity contracts. Likely single-family homes (omakotitalo) with higher than normal consumption.
- **Campaign goal:** branding and branded search, not clicks. YouTube clicks are hard to get.
- **CTA:** tell the viewer to search for Voltikka ("Hae: Voltikka" or similar).

## Constraints

- Brand rules in `design/voltikka-brand-guide.md`, `design/voltikka-design-system.md` and `PRODUCT.md` apply: plain Finnish, no hype or urgency, no exaggerated savings claims, the fox never next to a seller, contract or price recommendation, no named sellers, never mention Energiavirasto.
- Only claims Voltikka can prove: whole market, independent and commission-free, prices recalculated over all known price phases, hidden price increases labelled. No testimonials, user numbers, ratings or awards.
- Skippable in-stream: the skip button shows at 5 s. The name Voltikka and the hook must land before 5 s.
- Must work with sound off; a voice-over (if any) says "Voltikka" aloud to teach the search word.

## Process

Brief → message → technical spike → 3–4 concepts in Paper → storyboard and script in Paper → animatic → final motion and render.

### Bounded source-language coverage

`EnergyRuleSourceProof::validate(output, input, validator)` is one dedicated closed grammar helper.
It uses actual normalized source input, not the model's quote alone. Every short/long and extra-information
field is scanned at complete sentence/semicolon boundaries. Decimal points, email addresses and ISO dates
are not split. Quotes must cover complete price clauses; terminal full stops/semicolons/exclamation marks may be omitted.
Clear non-pricing text can coexist: bounded energy/home marketing, energy-origin statements, ordering
instructions and contact/support categories (including phone numbers and specific help-only conditions).
Unknown text is not presumed harmless merely because it lacks price keywords. Unsupported pricing,
detached eligibility/conditions, negation, optional fixing or quote omission still prevent known proof.
No company/ID exceptions, general NLP, fuzzy source matching or external fetch is used. This extends
usefulness for realistic mixed descriptions, but does not claim arbitrary language or broad live coverage.

English scopes: General, Day, Night, Winter, Other-season. Finnish scopes: Yleissähkön, Päiväsähkön,
Yösähkön, Talvisähkön, Muun ajan sähkön. Each clause addresses exactly one tariff. A natural Finnish
General clause can omit Yleissähkön only when metering is General, pricing is not Spot and exactly one
energy slot exists, of source type General. Day/Night/Season stay explicitly named. Complete examples:

- `General normal energy tariff is adjustable and is currently 9 cents/kWh`
- `General energy price is the normal tariff minus 5 cents/kWh for the first 3 months`

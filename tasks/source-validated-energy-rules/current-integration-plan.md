price guarantee or premium anchor. A future announced 11 holds 11 when no valid new reference
exists; it never restores old 9. Changing or missing evidence cannot fabricate an observation date.

Cheap proves its first-month 7.49 guarantee. Its later 9.95 announcement can supply an explicit
actual-only continuation estimate, not a current normal quote, independent dated lock or measured
benefit. Oomi, Voima, Iin, Tyyni and Hehku remain Unknown for material source limits. This bounded
grammar does not support every seller sentence; qualifications are never removed to make proof pass.

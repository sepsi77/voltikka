# Voltikka brand guide

*Working draft, 16 October 2026.*

## 1. The name

**Voltikka** on **voltikka.fi**. The name and the domain do not change (user, 2026-10-16). Themed words never replace SEO titles, H1 text, URLs or the `| Voltikka` suffix.

## 2. The story: fox fires

The Finnish word for the northern lights, *revontulet*, means "the fox's fires" (*repo* is an old word for fox). An old story says that a fox runs over the fells and its tail throws sparks into the sky.

- The design must work **without** the story. A fox under the aurora in a Finnish winter landscape reads as "Finnish, night, light", which is enough.
- Tell the story once, in quiet places: the about page (`/tietoa`), the 404 page, maybe one footer line. Example: *"Revontulet = revon tulet. Vanhan tarinan mukaan kettu juoksee tuntureilla ja sen häntä lennättää kipinöitä taivaalle."*
- The story is often told as a Sámi story. Use only the fox and the sky. Do not use Sámi clothing, drums or other cultural symbols.

## 3. The fox

A slim red fox with a white tail tip and a hand-knitted spruce-green Nordic scarf. **Approved reference: `mascot/fox-v2.png`** (user, 2026-10-16). New poses are image-gen variations of it; no illustrator redraw.

- **Tail:** ordinary fur. Small green and violet aurora sparkles may drift from the tip. **Never a flame tail, and never a fox curled around a round shape** (too close to a known browser logo).
- **Where:** the hero of every page type, in a pose that fits the page (design spec §6). At most once per page.
- **Never** next to a specific contract, seller or company page, and never in a pose that seems to recommend a contract. Voltikka is neutral.
- **Never** a funny pose on bad news (prices rising, errors).
- A fox character name is optional. A role name such as "Voltikan kettu" keeps the attention on Voltikka.

## 4. Picture-book feel (user, 2026-10-16)

The site should feel like a beautifully drawn picture book, **without interfering with functionality**. Inspiration is the warm Finnish picture-book tradition (for example Mauri Kunnas), but **only the feel, never the style**:

- Take: busy, warm everyday Finnish scenes with small humorous details that reward a second look (sauna smoke, snow shovelling, laundry, a coffee pot in a lit window); fine ink outlines with soft watercolour; animal villagers doing ordinary things.
- Do not take: his characters, his dog people, his compositions or his drawing style. Never name the artist (or any living artist) in an image prompt; describe the traits instead.
- The fox is the only main character and the only fox. Villagers (hare, badger, bear, owl and others) are tiny background extras in hero scenes, never near contracts, prices, sellers or controls, and never the focus. Exception (user, 2026-10-16): a villager interlude strip may be the focus of a pause between page blocks, for example between the contract list and its pagination and the "Katso myös" links. It never sits inside or next to a contract entry. On contract and company pages the fox stands at the far edge of the hero band, away from the contract name, price and logo. Never draw tents, tepees, traditional costumes or cultural symbols in maps or landscapes.

## 5. Voice

Plain, short Finnish. The number comes first. Say what a number is compared with ("tavallisesti 0,36 €", "vertailuna 30 päivän keskiarvo"). Estimates are marked "arvio". At most one light line per screen. Never reference Energiavirasto in public copy.

# Link magnet page ideas for Voltikka

Voltikka’s strongest link-magnet opportunity is to publish data-backed pages that answer what is really happening in Finnish electricity pricing. The best pages should be useful for consumers, citeable by journalists, and easy to discuss in social media communities such as Reddit.

## Strategic angle

The pages should not be generic electricity-contract landing pages. They should feel like public-interest data products:

- real Finnish electricity price and contract data
- clear comparison between contract types, companies, regions, or household profiles
- a simple headline insight or ranking
- charts/tables that are easy to cite
- enough methodology transparency that journalists and data-curious readers trust them

A useful framing:

> Data-backed electricity market explainers that show what Finnish households actually pay, how contract types differ, and how prices change over time.

## Page ideas

### 1. Sähkösopimusten hintakartta Suomessa

**Idea:** Municipality-level view of contract prices and availability.

**Possible headline:**

> Missä kunnassa saa halvimmat sähkösopimukset?

**Data to use:**

- cheapest available contract by municipality or postcode
- average of the cheapest 5 contracts
- contract availability by company
- differences between urban and rural areas

**Why it could attract links:**

Local and regional media often like municipality comparisons. The page could produce stories such as “Tampereella sähkösopimus maksaa X euroa vähemmän kuin…” and generate local discussion.

---

### 2. Pörssisähkö vs määräaikainen: kumpi olisi voittanut?

**Idea:** Show when spot electricity would have beaten fixed-price contracts historically.

**Possible headline:**

> Kuinka usein pörssisähkö oikeasti voittaa määräaikaisen sopimuksen?

**Data to use:**

- historical spot prices
- current and historical fixed contract prices
- consumption profiles, for example apartment, detached house, electric heating, EV household
- monthly and yearly win-rate by profile

**Why it could attract links:**

This is one of the most debated Finnish electricity topics. It is ideal for Reddit, forums, journalists, and search because it addresses a real argument with data instead of opinion.

---

### 3. Määräaikaisen sähkösopimuksen ajoitusindeksi

**Idea:** Track whether now is historically a good or bad time to lock in a fixed-term contract.

**Possible headline:**

> Kannattaako sähkösopimus sitoa nyt vai odottaa?

**Data to use:**

- fixed-term contract prices over time
- spot averages
- seasonality
- current fixed prices compared with the last 12 or 24 months

**Possible output:**

A simple score, for example:

> 12 kuukauden määräaikaiset sopimukset ovat nyt halvempia kuin 68 % päivistä viimeisen vuoden aikana.

**Why it could attract links:**

It turns uncertainty into a simple, shareable signal. Consumers search for this question constantly, and journalists can cite the index when writing about electricity prices.

---

### 4. Sähkösopimusten marginaaliseuranta

**Idea:** Track supplier margins, especially pörssisähkö margins and monthly fees.

**Possible headline:**

> Sähköyhtiöiden marginaalit ovat muuttuneet näin paljon.

**Data to use:**

- spot margins by provider
- monthly fees
- margin changes over time
- cheapest and most expensive supplier margins
- distribution of margins across the market

**Why it could attract links:**

The page creates accountability. Journalists and consumers are interested in how much electricity companies charge on top of the market price.

---

### 5. Sähköyhtiöiden hintamuutosvahti

**Idea:** A public changelog of contract price changes.

**Possible headline:**

> Mitkä sähköyhtiöt nostivat tai laskivat hintojaan tällä viikolla?

**Data to use:**

- new contracts
- removed contracts
- price increases and decreases
- promotions added or removed
- company-level weekly summaries

**Why it could attract links:**

This could become a recurring citation source and social content engine. It gives Voltikka a reason to publish weekly updates that people can share.

---

### 6. Sähkön hintapiikit ja arkielämän kustannukset

**Idea:** Translate spot-price spikes into concrete household costs.

**Possible headline:**

> Näin paljon sauna, sähköauto tai lämminvesivaraaja maksoi vuoden kalleimpana päivänä.

**Data to use:**

- spot price history
- appliance consumption assumptions
- highest and lowest hours, days, and months
- VAT-inclusive Finnish prices

**Why it could attract links:**

This makes electricity prices tangible. “c/kWh” is abstract, but “joulusauna maksoi X euroa” is understandable and media-friendly.

---

### 7. Sähköauton latauskustannusindeksi

**Idea:** Monthly tracker for home EV charging costs under different contract types.

**Possible headline:**

> Paljonko sähköauton kotilataus maksaa Suomessa juuri nyt?

**Data to use:**

- spot, fixed, and hybrid contract prices
- typical EV consumption per 100 km
- monthly driving profiles
- night-charging scenario

**Why it could attract links:**

EV owners are active online, and the topic overlaps electricity, cars, household budgeting, and climate.

---

### 8. Omakotitalon sähkölämmityksen riski-indeksi

**Idea:** Compare contract types specifically for electrically heated detached houses.

**Possible headline:**

> Kuinka paljon sopimusvalinta vaikuttaa sähkölämmitteisen omakotitalon vuosikustannukseen?

**Data to use:**

- 18,000–25,000 kWh consumption profiles
- winter-weighted usage
- contract-type comparison
- worst-case month modeling

**Why it could attract links:**

The financial stakes are high for electrically heated homes, especially before winter. This gives the page strong emotional and practical relevance.

---

### 9. Sähkösopimusten tarjous- ja kampanjaseuranta

**Idea:** Track how common electricity contract campaigns and discounts are.

**Possible headline:**

> Sähköyhtiöiden tarjoukset ovat lisääntyneet näin paljon.

**Data to use:**

- number of discounted contracts
- average value of discounts
- campaign duration
- common campaign types, such as 0 € monthly fee
- provider ranking by campaign activity

**Why it could attract links:**

It exposes marketing patterns in the electricity market. Useful for consumers and interesting for business/media analysis.

---

### 10. Sähkösopimusten läpinäkyvyysvertailu

**Idea:** Rank electricity companies by how understandable and transparent their pricing is.

**Possible headline:**

> Minkä sähköyhtiön hinnoittelu on helpointa ymmärtää?

**Data to use:**

- number of price components
- clarity of discounts
- whether monthly fee, margin, duration, and conditions are machine-readable
- contract complexity score

**Why it could attract links:**

This is editorial and potentially provocative. Companies may respond, users may share, and journalists may cite it when discussing consumer protection.

---

### 11. Sähkön hinta jouluna, juhannuksena ja lomakausina

**Idea:** Seasonal electricity price statistics for culturally relevant dates.

**Possible headline:**

> Milloin joulusauna on ollut kallein?

**Data to use:**

- spot prices by holiday
- appliance costs
- historical comparisons
- year-over-year holiday price changes

**Why it could attract links:**

Seasonal stories are easy PR. The same page can be refreshed and pitched every year before Christmas, midsummer, winter holidays, and other timely moments.

---

### 12. Pörssisähkön tuntiriski

**Idea:** Measure how volatile spot electricity is right now.

**Possible headline:**

> Kuinka suuri riski pörssisähkössä on tällä viikolla?

**Data to use:**

- price volatility
- number of expensive hours
- cheapest daily windows
- comparison to previous weeks and months
- extreme-hour count above selected thresholds

**Why it could attract links:**

It works as recurring social content. “This week’s spot risk is high/low” is simple to understand and share.

### 13. Sähkön hintaennätykset

**Idea:** A dedicated all-time records dashboard for the Finnish spot market.

**Possible headline:**

> Kallein tunti, halvin tunti ja pisin negatiivisten hintojen putki Suomen sähkömarkkinoilla.

**Data to use:**

- highest and lowest single hour ever recorded
- most expensive day, week, month, year
- longest negative-price streak
- count of negative-price hours per year
- count of hours above selected thresholds (50, 100, 500 c/kWh)
- year-by-year extreme value table

**Why it could attract links:**

Records are inherently screenshot-friendly. Every time a spike or a sunny windy weekend happens, journalists need a citation source for "the most expensive/cheapest ever" claims. Updates automatically without editorial work.

---

### 14. "Mitä sähkösi olisi maksanut?" hindsight-laskuri

**Idea:** Let a user upload their own Fingrid Datahub hourly consumption CSV and replay it against real historical contracts.

**Possible headline:**

> Lataa kulutustietosi ja näe, paljonko olisit maksanut pörssisähköllä, määräaikaisella tai hybridillä.

**Data to use:**

- user-supplied hourly consumption from Datahub
- historical hourly spot prices
- historical fixed-price contract levels (cheapest, median)
- per-year and per-month breakdown of what each contract type would have cost
- a clear winner per period

**Why it could attract links:**

This is the strongest viral candidate. The output is personal, surprising, and ends a recurring argument with concrete numbers. People share screenshots of their own results, which compound visibility on Reddit, X, and Finnish forums. The Datahub CSV format is standardized so the input cost for users is low.

---

### 15. Tuntihinta-lämpökartta

**Idea:** A 24 × 12 heatmap of average spot price by hour of day and month of year.

**Possible headline:**

> Milloin sähkö on ollut keskimäärin halvinta? Katso tunti tunnilta.

**Data to use:**

- multi-year hourly spot prices
- average, median, and percentile values per (hour, month) cell
- toggle between recent year, last 5 years, and all-time
- VAT-inclusive Finnish prices

**Why it could attract links:**

A single dense image carries the whole story, which is ideal for Twitter and embeds. It also has practical "when to run sauna/dishwasher/EV charger" utility, so it earns shares from both data nerds and ordinary consumers.

---

### 16. Negatiivisten hintojen seuranta

**Idea:** A live tracker for negative spot price hours.

**Possible headline:**

> Kuinka usein sähkö on ollut ilmaista tai miinusmerkkistä Suomessa?

**Data to use:**

- count of negative-price hours this year vs. last year
- longest current and historical streak
- calendar heatmap of negative hours
- breakdown by season and time of day
- typical weather conditions when negative prices occur

**Why it could attract links:**

Negative prices are counterintuitive and trend on social media every time they happen, especially during sunny windy weekends. A live page becomes the default citation source.

---

### 17. Aurinkopaneelien takaisinmaksuaika -kartta

**Idea:** A Finland map colored by solar panel payback period in years, computed by municipality.

**Possible headline:**

> Missä päin Suomea aurinkopaneelit maksavat itsensä takaisin nopeimmin?

**Data to use:**

- PVGIS production estimates by municipality coordinates
- current cheapest spot and fixed contract prices
- typical system size and installation cost
- self-consumption assumption
- regional ranking table next to the map

**Why it could attract links:**

A colored Finland map is one of the most reliably shareable visual formats in Finnish media. Combines two of the site's existing data sources (PVGIS + contract feed) into a single visual story.

---

## Recommended priorities

If the goal is publicity, links, and social discussion, prioritize these first:

1. **Pörssisähkö vs määräaikainen: kumpi olisi voittanut?**  
   Highest discussion potential because it answers an ongoing consumer debate.

2. **Sähköyhtiöiden hintamuutosvahti**  
   Best recurring content engine and useful for weekly social/news updates.

3. **Määräaikaisen sähkösopimuksen ajoitusindeksi**  
   Strong consumer decision hook and easy to cite.

4. **Sähkösopimusten marginaaliseuranta**  
   Good accountability and data-journalism angle.

5. **Omakotitalon sähkölämmityksen riski-indeksi**  
   High emotional and financial relevance for a large, engaged audience.

## Common structure for each page

Each link-magnet page should ideally include:

- one strong headline claim
- one hero chart or index
- latest update date
- methodology section
- downloadable or citeable data when possible
- shareable summary cards
- internal links to contract comparison pages
- JSON-LD structured data where appropriate
- a short “for journalists” citation block

## Positioning principle

Do not just create more SEO landing pages. Create citeable electricity-market data products with a clear public-interest angle.

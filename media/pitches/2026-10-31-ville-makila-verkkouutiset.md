## Backup figures (send only if he replies)

Households: 2,000 / 5,000 / 18,000 kWh (the last with electric heating). Prices include VAT. Spot = spot price + median margin 0.5 c/kWh + median monthly fee.

| | 2,000 kWh | 5,000 kWh | 18,000 kWh |
|---|---|---|---|
| Today's 12 kk fixed (11.80 c + 4.89 €/kk), €/year | 295 € | 649 € | 2,183 € |
| Spot average needed for 12 kk fixed to win | 11.5 c | 11.4 c | 11.3 c |
| Most expensive 12 months since 2023 (Nov 2023–Oct 2024) | 7.6 c | 7.6 c | 8.4 c |
| Spot, last 12 months (realized) | 209 € | 440 € | 1,563 € |
| Spot at futures prices, Nov 2026–Oct 2027 | 244 € | 528 € | 1,924 € |
| Choice on 21.1.: spot / 12 kk fixed, costs to 24.10. | 158 / 159 € | 330 / 335 € | 1,124 / 1,017 € |

- Spot was cheaper than today's 12 kk fixed in all 34 rolling 12-month periods since January 2023. 2022 is the exception (about 19 c/kWh).
- The 24 kk fixed (10.71 c + 4.50 €/kk) needs a spot average of about 10.2 c to win.
- Honest limit: a household that locked a fixed price in January did as well as spot, and better with electric heating. A fixed contract pays when it is cheap, not when fear is highest.
- Futures are the market price, not a forecast. Do not present them as a forecast.

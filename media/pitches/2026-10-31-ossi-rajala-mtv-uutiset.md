# Pitch: Ossi Rajala, MTV Uutiset

Status: draft, not sent. Figures: local database (statistics 2026-10-23, spot 2026-10-24, futures 2026-10-22). Script: `scripts/spot_vs_fixed.py`, sections 1 and 4.

- **Why him:** his analysis of 24.3.2025 said that Finns must either pay for stable supply or carry the risk of a swinging price. A household makes the same choice when it picks a fixed contract or spot. We can give the price of that certainty.
- **The figure:** in January, certainty was almost free. Now a 12-month fixed contract costs about 120 €/year more than spot at futures prices (5,000 kWh), and about 260 € with electric heating.
- **Send:** now. Not in the same week as the Egutkina pitch (also MTV).

## Backup figures (send only if he replies)

Prices include VAT. Spot = spot price + median margin 0.5 c/kWh + median monthly fee.

| | 2,000 kWh | 5,000 kWh | 18,000 kWh (heating) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Choice on 21.1.: spot / 12 kk fixed, costs to 24.10. | 158 / 159 € | 330 / 335 € | 1,124 / 1,017 € |
| Today's 12 kk fixed, €/year | 295 € | 649 € | 2,183 € |
| Spot at futures prices, Nov 2026–Oct 2027 | 244 € | 528 € | 1,924 € |
| Price of certainty, €/year | 51 € | 120 € | 258 € |

- The futures curve is the market price on 2026-10-22, not a forecast. Futures did not predict last winter well. Present it as "the market price of next year", not as a prediction.
- Electric heating: the futures put a high price on January 2027 (118 €/MWh). A heated house uses much electricity then, so spot looks less cheap for it.
- With electric heating, the January choice favoured fixed by about 107 € to 24.10.

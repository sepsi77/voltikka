# Finnish journalists who write about electricity prices

Date: 2026-10-31

Purpose: a working list for personalised outreach of Voltikka data stories. Each entry gives the story types the person writes, the articles that show it, and the hook to use in a personal opening line.

## Sources and confidence

This list merges three AI research reports in this folder:

- **[C]** `chat-gpt-deep-research-report.md`. It gives article titles, dates and contact data with citations. It is the most reliable source.
- **[G]** `gemini-journalist-research-report.md`. Many names have no article citation. Some claims about Voltikka in it are wrong (for example "444 contracts", "92 % spike accuracy", an "AI prediction tool"). Do not reuse its Voltikka claims.
- **[T]** `finnish-electricity-price-media-meta-topics.md`. It gives 19 story angles and many article links, but no bylines.

Confidence:

- **High**: an article by this person is cited, and the beat agrees with it.
- **Medium**: only a beat description or author bio is cited, or the article is old.
- **Low**: no cited article. Verify the person and the beat before you write.

**Before you send a pitch, open the article you refer to and confirm the byline.** All three reports are AI output. A wrong article in a personal opening line does more damage than a generic opening line. Contact data is from the reports and is not checked.

Pitch references (P1–P6) are the pitches in `../2026-10-21-story-pitches.md`:

- **P1** Fixed prices rose 44 % while spot was record cheap.
- **P2** One year after the bait-pricing decision, low starting prices are still sold (anniversary 2026-10-31).
- **P3** Open-ended customers pay up to double for the same electricity.
- **P4** Monthly contract-market scorecard.
- **P5** Do sellers pass on falls as fast as rises (not ready).
- **P6** One year of 15-minute prices.

The pitch file says: do not pitch our own forecasts, the daily spot alert, or data-centre, grid-fee and nuclear stories. The hooks below obey this, also where the AI reports suggest other angles.

# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Voltikka is for Finnish people making informed household energy decisions. Primary users are consumers comparing electricity contracts, checking spot prices to time energy-intensive appliances, estimating solar panel return on investment, or evaluating whether a heat pump would lower their energy use and bill.

Users often arrive with low trust in the broader comparison niche because many affiliate sites push contracts based on commission. They need clarity, complete coverage, transparent calculations, and confidence that recommendations are based on data rather than hidden incentives.

## Product Purpose

Voltikka helps Finnish households understand and optimize energy costs through contract comparison, spot-price visibility, solar production estimates, and heat pump savings calculations.

The product exists to make energy decisions easier, more transparent, and more evidence-based. It uses comprehensive Finnish electricity contract data, including the same source used by Energiavirasto, then makes that data more useful through filtering, cost calculation, context, and household-specific tools.

Success means users can quickly see their realistic options, understand the tradeoffs, and trust why one option may be better for their situation.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, practical.

Voltikka should feel like a competent Finnish energy advisor: calm, plain-spoken, data-literate, and on the user's side. It can be modern and energetic, but never hype-driven. The tone should make complex energy choices feel manageable without hiding caveats or uncertainty.

## Anti-references

Voltikka must not feel like a scammy affiliate comparison site, a commission-first lead-generation funnel, or a fake-neutral ranking page. Avoid patterns that create suspicion: exaggerated savings claims, overly aggressive CTAs, vague ranking logic, dark-pattern urgency, and promotional layouts where the highest-paying provider appears to be the best by default.

It should also avoid feeling bank-like and cold, utility-company corporate, crypto/neon, generic SaaS, or visually loud in a way that competes with the data.

## Design Principles

1. Trust before conversion. Explain sources, assumptions, and ranking logic clearly enough that users understand why results appear as they do.
2. Data earns the user's attention. Prices, savings, timing, emissions, and assumptions should be more prominent than decorative UI.
3. Make the complete market usable. Voltikka's advantage is broad coverage of contracts sold in Finland, so filtering, sorting, and comparison must make that breadth feel empowering rather than overwhelming.
4. Show uncertainty honestly. Calculators should communicate estimates, ranges, assumptions, and user inputs without pretending to be exact where they are not.
5. Help people act at the right level. A user should know the next useful step, whether that is comparing a contract, timing appliance use, testing solar ROI, or estimating heating savings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA as the baseline. Interfaces should work well for keyboard users, screen readers, reduced motion preferences, and users with color-vision differences. Energy decisions affect all households, so the language should stay plain, Finnish-first, and understandable without specialist knowledge.
